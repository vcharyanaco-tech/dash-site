    Frequently Asked Questions

    • Both Apple Pencil (USB-C) and Apple Pencil (1st generation) are compatible with this iPad.

      Apple Pencil (USB-C) pairs via USB-C Charge Cable and attaches magnetically for storage.

      To use Apple Pencil (1st generation) with this iPad, a USB-C to Apple Pencil Adapter is required for pairing and charging. It is included with your purchase of Apple Pencil (1st generation). If you already own an Apple Pencil (1st generation), you’ll need to purchase the USB-C to Apple Pencil Adapter. This adapter is sold separately at Apple Store locations, on apple.com and on the Apple Store app.

    • iPad includes eSIM technology that enables fast, convenient and secure activation of mobile data plans. There are a few ways to activate eSIM on your iPad, including transferring from another iPad, joining an existing iPhone data plan, adding a new plan in Settings, scanning a QR code or using an app from a network provider. Footnote 1 With eSIM, you can choose mobile data plans from selected network providers in more than 190 countries and regions around the world. Learn more about setting up eSIM.

    • Magic Keyboard Folio is designed specifically for iPad. Third-party keyboards are also available.

      Explore iPad Keyboards

    • Apple accepts most credit and debit cards, Net Banking, UPI, and Payment on Delivery. You can also pay in instalments with EMI using an eligible credit or debit card.

      During Checkout, you can compare instalment options and select the one that works best for you.

      Some payment options may not be available for all products. Subject to card eligibility. Learn more about Apple payment (opens in new window), as well as Terms and Conditions (opens in new window). You can call 00800 040 1966 for more information.

    • AppleCare+ for iPad is an insurance product that provides expert technical support and hardware coverage for iPad, Apple Pencil and Apple‑branded iPad keyboard, including unlimited incidents of accidental damage protection. Each incident is subject to an excess fee of ₹2,500 for screen repair, ₹8,900 for other accidental damage for iPad models launched after 1 May 2024, ₹3,900 for accidental damage to all other iPad models, and ₹2,500 for Apple Pencil or Apple‑branded iPad keyboard. The screen repair service fee is only available on eligible iPad models when there is no other damage present. For the list of eligible models, please refer to the terms (opens in new window). Coverage begins on your AppleCare+ purchase date.
