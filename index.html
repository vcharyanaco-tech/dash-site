What the application does

India Post Dashboard turns the Circle Office's operational data into a live, role-based workspace for authorized India Post personnel.

Manage records

Track operational records and statuses in real time across the circle, with search, filters, and exportable reports.

Track submissions

Monitor submissions and tasks, with review reminders and approval workflows to keep work moving.

Generate reports

Produce operational reports and analytics from live data for review and decision-making.

Stay accountable

Every action is recorded in a complete audit log, keeping a transparent trail for the organization.

Who can use it

Access is controlled by role-based login and permission groups.

The application is intended exclusively for authorized staff of the Circle Office, Haryana, India Post. Access is controlled by role-based login and permission groups. It is not offered to the general public and does not display advertising.

Google services used

The application is built on Google Workspace and uses the minimum Google services required to function.

Google Sheets

Reads and writes records in the organization-owned dashboard spreadsheet (records, submissions, tasks, approvals, audit log).

Google Drive

Stores documents attached to records and temporary report and export files. Only files created or opened by the application are accessed.

Identity

Uses the user's email address to identify the signed-in user, apply role-based permissions, and record an audit trail of actions.

No user data is sold, rented, or shared for advertising. Full details in the Privacy Policy Terms of Service Contact Support
